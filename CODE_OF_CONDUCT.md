# Code of Conduct

We want Hunterly to be a welcoming project. Contributors and maintainers pledge to treat everyone with respect, regardless of experience, background, identity, or opinion.

**Expected:** be kind and constructive, give and accept feedback gracefully, assume good intent, focus on what is best for the project.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that break these rules, and may ban repeat offenders. To report a problem, email rishu24oct@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
